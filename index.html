We work with smallholder farmers, cooperatives, and small businesses in Tanzania on climate finance. We run financial literacy training, lend through a microfinance practice licensed by the Central Bank of Tanzania, and do the modelling and risk work for funding proposals. In January 2026 we trained more than 280 women in Isajilo Ward, Rungwe. Two proposals in the public pipeline come to US$1.7 billion.
